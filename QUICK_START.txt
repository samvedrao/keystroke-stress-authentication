# ⚡ QUICK START (2 Minutes)

## 🚀 Start the App

```bash
python app_auth.py
```

Then open: **http://localhost:5000**

---

## 📝 Register (First Time)

1. Click **"Register"** tab
2. Enter:
   - **Username**: anything you want
   - **Password**: any password
   - **Email**: (skip it, optional)
3. Click **"Register"**

✅ **You're logged in!**

---

## ⌨️ Type Naturally

1. Click **"⌨️ Analyze"** button (top right)
2. **Just type** - stats update live:
   - Speed
   - Hold time
   - Consistency

**That's it!** You're done. Typing is analyzed.

---

## 🎯 Optional: Get Predictions

1. After typing 3+ characters
2. Click **"📊 Optional: Analyze"** if you want stress analysis
3. See if typing is regular or stressed

---

## 📊 View Your History

Click **"📊 Dashboard"** to see:
- Your typing speed trend
- Session distribution chart
- Recent typing sessions

---

## 👤 Your Profile

Click **"👤 Profile"** to see:
- Your account info
- Your statistics
- Account settings

---

## 🚪 Logout

Click **"🚪 Logout"** when done.

Next time, click **"Login"** tab instead of "Register".

---

**That's all you need to know!** 🎉

Everything else is optional. Enjoy! ⌨️
