🛠️ TROUBLESHOOTING:

Q: All models show as "not loaded"?
A: Models are now properly loaded. Check if Flask was restarted.

Q: Typing interface not responding?
A: Clear input with "Reset" button, refresh page, type fresh.

Q: Predictions not working?
A: Ensure you've typed at least 5 characters before analyzing.

Q: Flask server not running?
A: Restart with: python app.py (in keystroke_project directory)

Q: Can't access dashboard?
A: Check http://localhost:5000 is correct, Flask may have crashed
